I rise in firm support of the motion: There needs to be strict laws to regulate Large Language Models. This is not a matter of stifling innovation, but a critical imperative for public safety, democratic integrity, and ethical accountability. Let me present my case.

First, the scale of potential harm is immense and already materializing. Without strict regulation, LLMs can and do generate highly convincing disinformation at an industrial scale, undermining elections and public trust. They can be weaponized to create sophisticated phishing campaigns, malicious code, and detailed plans for illegal activities. The absence of a legal framework means there is no consistent accountability for these harms, leaving society vulnerable to bad actors.

Second, the foundational data and inherent biases of these systems demand legal oversight. LLMs are trained on vast datasets scraped from the internet, often without consent, raising serious copyright and privacy concerns. More dangerously, they absorb and amplify societal biases, leading to discriminatory outputs in hiring, lending, and law enforcement. Only strict laws can mandate transparency in training data, enforce rigorous bias auditing, and establish redress mechanisms for those harmed.

Third, the existential and societal risks are too profound to leave to voluntary corporate governance. The rapid, unchecked development of increasingly powerful AI could lead to unforeseen systemic risks, including the erosion of human agency and the destabilization of labor markets. Corporate interests, driven by profit and competition, cannot be trusted to self-regulate against these dangers. History shows that transformative technologies—from automobiles to pharmaceuticals—require robust regulatory frameworks to ensure they serve the public good.

Therefore, I argue that strict, proactive legislation is essential. This must include mandatory safety testing before public release, clear liability frameworks for AI-caused harm, strict transparency requirements, and independent oversight bodies. The goal is to harness the incredible potential of LLMs while erecting essential guardrails that protect our society, our democracy, and our shared future. To do otherwise is an unacceptable gamble with our collective security. The time for strict regulation is now.
